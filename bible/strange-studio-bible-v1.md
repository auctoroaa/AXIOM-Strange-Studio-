# Strange Studio Bible v1

**Status:** Consolidated design index, not a claim of a completed product.

## Identity

**Strange Studio** is an Observable Intelligence Operating System. Tagline: **See. Think. Build.** The environment should explain the system rather than conceal it.

## Canonical principles

- Truth over theater.
- Observable state must correspond to source-backed events or be labeled illustrative.
- Cause, dependency, and outcome should be inspectable.
- Unknown is better than invented certainty.
- Concepts, specifications, implementation, and verification are separate states.
- Verification gates delivery.
- Accessibility remains available alongside spatial metaphors.
